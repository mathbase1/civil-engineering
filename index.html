Quick refresher (core equations)
  • Standard form: \(ax^2+bx+c=0\)
  • Quadratic formula: \(\displaystyle x=\frac{-b\pm\sqrt{b^2-4ac}}{2a}\)
  • Discriminant: \(\Delta=b^2-4ac\) (real roots when \(\Delta\ge 0\)).
  • Engineering rule: if you get two real roots, choose the physically meaningful one (positive time/depth, station in range, “left side”, etc.).
